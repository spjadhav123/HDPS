Privacy Policy

For the Humpty Dumpty Preschool Mobile Application

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

Humpty Dumpty Preschool ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Humpty Dumpty Preschool mobile application (Package Name: com.nashik.humpty_dumpty_preschool). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application and the choices you have associated with that data.

We use your data to provide and improve the Application services. By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

Types of Data Collected:


  • Personal Data: While using our Application, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including:
    • Email address
    • First name and last name
    • Phone number
    • User Role (Admin, Teacher, Accountant, Parent)
    • Child's name, class, and division (for Parent accounts)
  • Media and Files: With your permission, the Application allows you to upload photos, videos, and documents (such as PDFs, Excel sheets, and CSV files) to facilitate:
    • Preschool activity galleries
    • Student academic reports and notifications
    • Student assignments and teacher worksheets
  • Financial Information: If you perform fee payments or billing transactions within the App, these payments are processed securely through our payment gateway provider, Razorpay. We do not store your credit card, debit card, or banking credentials on our servers. Razorpay processes and stores this payment data in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information on how the Application is accessed and used. This may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, device model, operating system version, page visits within the app, time spent on pages, and diagnostic logs.

2. Device Permissions Required

To provide full features, the Application requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera Permission: Required to take photos or videos of school events, student activities, or documents for direct upload to the app.
  • Photo Library & Storage: Required to select existing photos/videos/documents from your device, or to save generated reports (like student progress sheets) to your local storage.
  • Network/Internet Access: Required to sync application data with our backend Firebase servers and process real-time notifications.

3. Service Providers & Third-Party Integration

We employ third-party companies to facilitate our services, perform service-related tasks, or assist us in analyzing how our Application is used. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication (Firebase Auth), database hosting (Cloud Firestore), and media/document storage (Firebase Storage). Data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies.
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to track app usage, crash reports, and performance metrics to help us fix issues and improve the app layout.
  • Razorpay: Used as the payment processor for fee collection. Their Privacy Policy governs the use of your payment details.

4. Children's Privacy

Important Note on Children's Data:

Our Application is designed for use by parents, teachers, and school administrators. We do not collect personal data directly from children. Parents or legal guardians provide the names and details of their children to the preschool management system so we can provide educational reports and tracking services. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data directly from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

5. Data Retention and Security

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, including Firebase encryption in transit and at rest.

We will retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy (for example, while your child is enrolled in our preschool).

6. User Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. To request deletion, you can:

  • Submit an account deletion request within the App Settings (if available).
  • Or contact the school administration directly via email at support@humptydumpty.edu with your registered username/email.

Upon receiving your request, we will delete your account and associated personal data from our active databases within 14 business days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records (such as financial audit logs).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
